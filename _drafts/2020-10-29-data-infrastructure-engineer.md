Recently I was approached by a friend to give some suggestions for learning about data center operations, and since I have experience as a "big data infrastructure engineer", I thought I might as well write a post about some resources I found helpful during that time.

- [COURSE Google - System Administration and IT Infrastructure Services](https://www.coursera.org/instructor/google)
- [COURSE Google - Technical Support Fundamentals](https://www.coursera.org/instructor/google)
