I just put here some references on the topic, but in general, we can use control theory for the continous-time setting, or discretize and use Reinforcement Learning techniques. However, some RL techniques have been proposed to work directly on the continuous-time setting (See [4] for example).

References:
- Bertsekas, Dynamic Programming and Optimal Control Vol II, 5 Continuous-time Problems
- [Continuous-time Markov decision process](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Markov_decision_process#Continuous-time_Markov_decision_process)
- [Hamilton–Jacobi–Bellman equation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamilton%E2%80%93Jacobi%E2%80%93Bellman_equation)
- Bradtke, Steven J., and Michael O. Duff. "Reinforcement learning methods for continuous-time Markov decision problems." Advances in neural information processing systems (1995): 393-400.
