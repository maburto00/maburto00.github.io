Since some time ago, I prefer to borrow books from the library or to have electronic versions instead of buying physical versions for financial reasons (I prefer to buy other things), but more importantly, for mobility (moving out is difficult when you own too many bulky things). Nevertheless, I miss the feeling of seeing the books I like in my bookshelf.. Even thought I use Goodreads for organizing what I have read I will try to put here  the books I would like to have in my bookshelf (with the advantage that I can remove them if I feel like it more easily), trying to keep it short (but no promises). I am sorry for the format, but I want to be practical so I will put just the most important information (title, author only probably most of the times). I will also mix spanish and english texts.
