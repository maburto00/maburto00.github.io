# Mendeley
## Advantages
- Free (no need to pay)
## Disadvantages
- Propietary software (may not be a disadvantage to many people)
- Highlights and annotations keep stored in Mendeley not the PDF (so you cannot send your annotations to others)

# Zotero
## Advantages
- Free (no need to pay)
- Open source software
- You open and annotate the PDF's in your favorite PDF Editor (so annotations are stored in the PDF and you can send them to others)
