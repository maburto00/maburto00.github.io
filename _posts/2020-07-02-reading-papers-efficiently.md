A little before starting my PhD I looked for many resources on how to read papers efficiently. I have read many resources, have tried many approaches, and now that I have read more papers I think I am getting better at it. However, I felt the need to create this post with two goals in mind:
- To have a summary of the main points I have learned to read papers efficiently 
- To gather all the resources I have consulted for reading effectively

The summary is probably more helpful to me than to anybody else, but I think you can get advantage mainly of the references if you are interested in improving your efficiency on reading papers.

# Time to spend

According to [2], there are people who spend 3 hours weekly on reading, some others 3 hours daily. 

Resources from where to get interesting papers
- Journal table of contents, Google Scholar Alerts, Journal Clubs, Journal Scan Club
- Reddit, Twitter, Facebook, Discord,

# Information overload
- Google alerts is a great tool, but must be used carefully to avoid information overflow [3]
